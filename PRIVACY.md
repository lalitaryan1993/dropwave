# DropWave privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 November 2026_

DropWave connects your own devices (PCs, phones, TVs) directly over your own Wi-Fi or a USB cable. It has **no server, no account, no ads and no analytics or tracking**. The developer never receives your files, messages, notifications, calls, contacts, photos or anything else.

## What stays on your devices

Everything DropWave shows or moves (files, notifications, SMS, call details, contacts, photos, clipboard, screen and camera pictures) goes **only between devices you have paired**, on your own network, encrypted where the connection supports it. Settings, history, trusted devices and the Vault are stored on your own devices and can be deleted from the app or by uninstalling it.

## When DropWave goes on the internet

Only in these cases, and never with your data:

- **Updates** (Windows app downloaded from GitHub): it checks GitHub Releases for a newer version. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store instead.
- **USB connection**: the first time you use it, the PC app downloads Google's free Android platform tools from `dl.google.com`.
- **Links you open**: if you tap a link or an app button (for example YouTube on the TV remote), that app or website opens on your device and its own privacy policy applies.

## Permissions on Android

The Android app asks for each permission only when you turn on the feature that needs it (for example notifications to show them on the PC, SMS to read and send texts from the PC, the camera to use the phone as a webcam). You can turn any of them off in Android settings at any time.

## Children

DropWave does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
